Scientific calculator

On every page · bottom-right button

Tap Calculator at the bottom right of any page to open a full scientific calculator beside your work.

  • It starts in DEG mode, ready for trigonometry.
  • 2nd then tan gives tan⁻¹, to find an angle.
  • ⌫ deletes one key, AC clears everything, Ans uses your last answer.
  • S⇔D shows the answer as a fraction. The clock shows your history.
