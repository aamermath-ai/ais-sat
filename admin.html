Connect

Paste the web-app address of your statistics script (it ends in /exec) and your admin key. They are saved only in this browser.

Teacher logins

Add a teacher’s email (school, Outlook/Microsoft or Gmail). That email is their username: they press Log in on the teacher page, type it, and get a 6-digit code by email. No passwords.

Worksheet requests

Contributors upload lesson files for their grades. Uploads wait in the queue until a Claude session builds the drafts (ask Claude to “build the queued worksheets”). The contributor checks the draft and sends it to you. Preview it, then Approve to make it live, or Request changes.
