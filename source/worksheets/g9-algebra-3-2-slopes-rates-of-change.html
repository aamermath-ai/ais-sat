Part B · Rise and run

Rise = change in y (up +, down −). Run = change in x (right +, left −). Type one number or a fraction like 11/7.

Part C · Use the slope formula

m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). Label the points first and keep the same order on top and bottom.

Part D · Think about slope

Steepness is the size of the number. Direction is the sign (+ or −).

Part E · Rate of change in the UAE

Rate of change = change in y ÷ change in x. Always say what it means, with units.
